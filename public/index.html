FlowPlanned, 2025 to now

A digital planner that behaves like a paper notebook. Everything stays on your machine, including the AI. Built because every planner I tried wanted an account before it would let me write a single line.

Basileia, 2023 to 2025

A personal care brand I bootstrapped from a kitchen. Fifteen products formulated by hand, trademark registered, supply quotes with resorts, orders shipped to five countries. I also ran soap-making workshops for women starting their own thing. I exited by transferring the stock and the business to another local founder instead of letting it go quiet.

Happy Zoned, 2024

Mobile sleeping pods for budget stays in Dubai. It ran straight into GCC zoning rules. I archived it rather than fight the regulation, and kept a very specific education in how regional compliance kills good ideas early.

The Nature Hub

A fifteen-zone agri-leisure and education destination for a golf resort. Spatial layouts, revenue models, vendor database, and event packages priced for real budgets. My favourite kind of problem: physical space, real constraints, no software.

Genista Biosciences

UX for a food-safety monitoring platform. Owned the design system and the analytics work, with hundreds of mockups tested and cut down over repeated rounds with actual lab users.

No-Code and Low-Code Era

Worked across Editor X, Locofy, and a handful of other companies building in this space. Active in the communities themselves too, not just the products. Answered roughly a hundred technical questions a week from a two thousand member developer community, and built free indie tools and templates for people in the space to use.

UX Design Studio, 2020 to 2021

Five web and mobile products designed end to end through lockdown, from user interviews to prototype. Learned how to run research on people you never meet in person.

Open Destinations, 2019 to 2020

Engineering on Tineri, a mobile itinerary platform for tour operators. Ionic 3 and Ruby on Rails, working on core modules of a real enterprise system.

IFB Industries

An auto on/off circuit for a washing machine. Microcontroller code that shut the display down when nobody was standing there, because idle power adds up across a million units.

BOLT IoT Evangelist

Taught IoT and machine learning to people who had never soldered anything, mostly by building the thing with them.
